Day 3 · stress-testing what the model gave you

The murderboard

Two models argue over a frozen artifact until both sign off on the identical bytes – or until the disagreement is recorded as the finding.

Who is who. The reviewers are two frontier models, run as separate sessions so neither can see the other. The author is you – the human who wrote the thing under review. You are not a third reviewer: you adjudicate. You rule on every contested point, you can reopen anything, and you are the only one who edits the artifact.

Reviewers never edit
A reviewer produces findings, never a fix – not even a demonstrably correct number. The author edits. A reviewer who revises the artifact has destroyed the independence that made the review worth having.
A GO is obtained, never inferred
Applying the other reviewer’s wording does not make their GO. Being obviously right does not make it. Every version needs an explicit verdict from each reviewer, even when the ruling looks foregone.
Refusal is a first-class outcome
A held position must be re-argued against the new input and must name what would change it. A refusal no evidence could overturn is a preference, not a finding.
A board that must end in agreement will produce agreement – and it will mean nothing.

Why it belongs in this workshop. On Day 3 a model will hand you a taxonomy that looks authoritative. The independence test catches one failure; the murderboard catches the rest – unsupported inference, missing denominators, circular nulls, unfalsifiable summary claims, self-validating evidence. On the case this method was written from, the board rewrote the paper’s thesis sentence. That is the point: it is not a quality gate at the end, it changes what the artifact is, early.

The machine-checked close. Generations are immutable and named by hash, so a verdict always describes bytes that still exist. CONDITIONAL_GO is not a state – a GO carrying an unmet condition is downgraded to NO_GO, because two different kinds of condition once wore the same label and were counted as agreement.

The honest limit. The drafter cannot be one of the two reviewers – a GO from whoever wrote it is a self-assessment, and no checker can tell the difference. Either bring in a third, or say plainly that dual GO is not available. Naming the arrangement is the requirement; the weaker arrangement is acceptable, the unnamed one is not.
